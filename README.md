<h1>Classroom of the Elite</h1>
<p> Classroom of the Elite is a Japanese light novel series written by Shōgo Kinugasa, with illustration by Shunsaku Tomose.</p>

<h2>Plot</h2>
<p>The Japanese government has established the Tokyo Metropolitan Advanced Nurturing School, dedicated to instruct and foster the generation of people that will support the country in the future. The students are given a high degree of freedom in order to closely mimic real life. The story follows the perspective of Kiyotaka Ayanokōji, a quiet and modest boy, who is bad at making friends and would rather keep his distance, but possesses unrivaled intelligence and incredible physical ability. He is a student of Class-D, which is where the school dumps its inferior students, widely considered "defective". After meeting Suzune Horikita and Kikyō Kushida, two other students in his class, the situation begins to change, and he gets involved in many affairs and his thought of an ideal normal high school life begins to get scattered, taking a toll on his sanity.</p>

<h2>Characters</h2>
<h3>Kiyotaka Ayanokoji</h3>
<p><strong>Kiyotaka Ayanokōji</strong> (綾あやの小こう路じ 清きよ隆たか, <em>Ayanokōji Kiyotaka</em>) is the main protagonist of the <a href="https://you-zitsu.fandom.com/wiki/Y%C5%8Dkoso_Jitsuryoku_Shij%C5%8D_Shugi_no_Ky%C5%8Dshitsu_e">You-Zitsu">You-Zitsu</a> series. He is currently a second-year student of <a href="https://you-zitsu.fandom.com/wiki/Advanced_Nurturing_High_School">Advanced Nurturing High School</a>. Following the entrance exam in which he scored precisely 50 points in every subject, he was placed in D-Class. In the anime adaptation, he sits at the rear left corner of the class seat next to Suzune Horikita.</p>

<h3>Suzune Horikita</h3>
<p><strong>Suzune Horikita</strong> (堀ほり北きた 鈴すず音ね, <em>Horikita Suzune</em>) is one of the main characters of the <a href="https://you-zitsu.fandom.com/wiki/Y%C5%8Dkoso_Jitsuryoku_Shij%C5%8D_Shugi_no_Ky%C5%8Dshitsu_e">You-Zitsu">You-Zitsu</a> series and the main heroine of the Horikita spin-off manga. She is known to be the younger sister of Manabu Horikita, the Student Council president of the school on the 1st Year Arc.
  
She is currently a second-year student of <a href="https://you-zitsu.fandom.com/wiki/Advanced_Nurturing_High_School">Advanced Nurturing High School</a>. At the start of the series, she was introduced as the class representative and a student of Class 1-D, seated at the rear row of the class, next to Kiyotaka Ayanokōji.</p>

<h3>Kei Karuizawa</h3>
<p><strong>Kei Karuizawa</strong> (軽かる井い沢ざわ 恵けい, <em>Karuizawa Kei</em>) is one of the heroines of the <a href="https://you-zitsu.fandom.com/wiki/Y%C5%8Dkoso_Jitsuryoku_Shij%C5%8D_Shugi_no_Ky%C5%8Dshitsu_e">You-Zitsu</a> series. Currently a second-year student of <a href="https://you-zitsu.fandom.com/wiki/Advanced_Nurturing_High_School">Advanced Nurturing High School</a>, she is a prominent figure in Class D and is considered to be the girl's leader of the class.</p>
